I graduated with Excellent Distinction in Data Science from the University of Science and Technology of Hanoi (USTH), completing my studies from 2023 to 2026.

During my final year, I had the opportunity to compete in the **Denso Hackathon 2025** as the team leader of **FPUS**. Our team reached the **Top 4** finalists, working on physical robotics solutions aimed at transforming traditional factories into smart, automated production environments. The competition, organized by DENSO Vietnam in partnership with FPT, brought together talented students to tackle real-world challenges in smart manufacturing.

![FPUS team at Denso Hackathon 2025](denso.png)

Beyond academics, I was actively involved in extracurricular activities. I served as a member of the USTH Student Union for three years, from 2023 to 2026 — an organization dedicated to organizing student activities and acting as a bridge between students and the university.
